# Wording pass: formal lines in the game, and simpler versions

**Approved by the user and applied on 2026-10-17** (all 62 rows; the tests that check these words were updated). The "Now" column is what the game said before.

Two more were changed the same day, at the user's go: the leaderboard tab "AMING PAARALAN" is now "SCHOOL NATIN", and "Panghuling Pagsubok (Boss):" on the era-complete screen is now "Boss level:".

The rule: everyday Taglish, the way students talk. English for app and game words (save, password, level, coins, gate, teacher), short Tagalog for the rest.

Left alone on purpose: the six era titles, the history paragraphs and the term cards, the quiz questions (the teacher's text), the Kaalaman name, and the short lines that are already natural ("TAMA!", "GALING MO!", "Sikreto mo ito, ha!", "Konti na lang!").

## One thing that is a mistake, not only style

Login messages tell the student to choose 'Bago' or 'Babalik'. Those were the old tab names. The buttons now say "BAGO AKO" and "BALIK NA AKO". (Rows 14 and 17.)
