ChronoQuest · GAME · mockup, nothing built yet

Six boss rooms, six different layouts

Each room is drawn wall to wall (2,736 units) at one scale, as it is now and as proposed, with its own boss and look. Every room has its own kind of structure, and none is taller than 4 tiles (96 units, under a jump's 128). Boulders on the shore, a fort gatehouse, a plaza dais, sandbag barricades, a collapsed bridge ramp, a marble ziggurat.

Where the question rune can land (new)Gold marks them: the floor and the top of any raised block 2 tiles wide or more. Today the rune lands only on the flat floor.
